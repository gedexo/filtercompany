Who we are

The The Filter Company is a water filter and purification company based in Calicut, Kerala, providing water treatment solutions for homes, businesses, and industries. We have supplied and installed RO, UV, UF, and alkaline purification systems for offices, hotels, institutions, residences, factories, and homes — helping our customers get safe, clean, healthy, and great-tasting water every day.

From domestic water purifiers to commercial purification systems, water treatment plants, and industrial water treatment solutions, we provide systems designed around the specific requirements of each application. Whether it is drinking water for a family, purified water for an office or hotel, or a larger treatment system for an industrial facility, we focus on understanding the requirements before recommending a suitable solution.

We provide reliable water solutions focused on quality, advanced purification technology, and customer satisfaction. From system selection and installation to maintenance and after-sales support, we deliver dependable solutions designed for performance, durability, and long-term use.
